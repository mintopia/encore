# Support Encore

Encore is free and open source, built and maintained in spare time. If it's playing music in
your home and you'd like to say thanks, you can sponsor its development on GitHub.

<p><a class="sponsor-btn" href="https://github.com/sponsors/mintopia">Sponsor on GitHub</a></p>

One-off and monthly sponsorships both help, and so does
[starring the repository](https://github.com/mintopia/encore) or
[reporting a bug](https://github.com/mintopia/encore/issues).

## Lyrics

The [lyrics overlay](/features/lyrics) gets its lyrics from [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net/), a
free, community-run lyrics database. If you use it, consider
[donating to LRCLIB](https://github.com/tranxuanthang/lrclib#donation) too.
